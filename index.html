About me

About Me

I'm Janvi Gabani, a data science graduate student at Northeastern University in Boston, concentrating in Data Modeling, Warehousing & Database Architecture, and a Teaching Assistant for Machine Learning. I hold a BE in Computer Engineering from Gujarat Technological University.

My experience spans research and product work. At ISRO's Space Applications Centre, I was responsible for analyzing satellite data in the Navigation Receivers Division, working on GNSS positioning and Kalman filtering in C. At Cybersecurity Umbrella, I built AI tools for task summarization and ticket classification, and contributed to the product's AI roadmap.

I'm drawn to the place where data meets real-world operations: manufacturing, continuous improvement, and digital transformation. I enjoy turning messy data into systems and insights that help teams work better.
